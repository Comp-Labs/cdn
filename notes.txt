The Power of Averaging & Trends:
•	KFC Example: If data shows a 20% spike in "Family Buckets" on Tuesday nights in suburban outlets, KFC can run a "Tuesday Treat" promo to lean into that existing momentum.
•	Big Bazaar Example: Using "Footfall vs. Conversion" stats. If 5,000 people enter but only 2,000 buy, the math suggests the "choke point" is either the price or the checkout line length.
•	RFM Analysis (The "Simple" Secret): Fintechs provide Recency, Frequency, and Monetary analysis.
•	Recency: When did they last visit?
•	Frequency: How often do they come?
•	Monetary: How much do they spend?
•	Multi-Outlet Management: For a CEO, the "math" is about standard deviation. Why is the Mumbai KFC branch 3x more efficient than the Pune one? The data highlights the outlier.

Inventory Optimization: Math tells them exactly how many buns to order so they don't rot (waste) but also don't run out (lost profit).
•	Staffing: If the POS data shows a consistent surge at 7:00 PM, the manager schedules more staff for that window. That’s statistics in action—minimizing "wait time" to maximize "customer satisfaction."
•	Predictive Sneak-Peeks (combined with other factors): Using historical data (Last year's Diwali sales) to predict this year's stock needs.
